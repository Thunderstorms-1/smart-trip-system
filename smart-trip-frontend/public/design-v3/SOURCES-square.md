# Square production assets

The lighthouse image and local display-font subset are byte-identical copies of the user-approved independent Square preview assets, migrated 2026-10-15 Asia/Shanghai. The original prototype remains under `/design-preview-v3/square.html`.

- `square-scenery.png`: user-supplied Image 2 (`codex-clipboard-0768280f-27be-488c-aade-10396051f900.png`), originally supplied 2026-10-14. Environmental hero imagery; no assertion of a real destination or post cover. PNG embedded provenance and `square-scenery.png.json` are retained.
- `square-title.woff2`: Ma Shan Zheng 400, the previously approved Google Fonts subset for “把世界变成你的下一段旅程”. Source: https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Ma+Shan+Zheng . Project/license: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/mashanzheng . Copyright 2018 The Ma Shan Zheng Project Authors. Included `OFL-MaShanZheng.txt` carries SIL Open Font License 1.1.
- Existing `login-scenery.png`: approved environmental imagery reused for the closing banner; its existing provenance is retained.

Only real community records populate production cards. The current feed has no cover field, so card media uses a neutral surface rather than stock or concept photos assigned to a city.
